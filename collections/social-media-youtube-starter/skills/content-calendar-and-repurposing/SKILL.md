# Content Calendar and Repurposing
- Confirm campaign dates, time zone, platforms, capacity and approvals.
- Plan pillars, series, seasonal moments and content mix.
- Build a calendar with date, platform, format, topic, hook, CTA, owner, status and assets.
- Extract distinct insights or moments from a source asset.
- Adapt hook, pacing, framing, captions, title and CTA for each platform; do not blindly repost.
- Include production buffer and weekly review.
Deliver a 2–4 week calendar and repurposing map. Never claim content was scheduled unless it was.
