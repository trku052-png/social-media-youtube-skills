# Instagram Growth and Reels
- Define audience, goal and profile conversion action.
- Audit name field, bio, profile image, link, highlights and pinned posts.
- Match format to purpose: Reels for discovery, carousels for education/saves, Stories for relationships.
- Write Reels with a strong opening, one narrative, visual progression, captions and CTA.
- Plan carousels slide-by-slide: promise, context, steps, example, recap and CTA.
- Review reach, non-follower reach, watch time, shares, saves, profile visits and follows when supplied.
Use account baselines; avoid guarantees.
