# Trend and Competitor Research
- Confirm niche, geography, language, platforms and research window.
- Search public and platform-native sources where accessible.
- Record direct URLs, account, date, format, topic and visible metrics.
- Compare posts to each account's baseline where possible; avoid raw-view comparisons without context.
- Separate observed facts, interpretations and recommendations.
- Identify audience questions, gaps and original opportunities.
Deliver findings with links, evidence caveats and original ideas. Never invent trends or metrics.
