# YouTube Channel Growth
- Request channel URL, niche, target viewer, goal and available analytics.
- Audit channel promise, profile, playlists, topic clusters and publishing consistency.
- Review impressions, CTR, views, average view duration, retention, traffic sources and returning viewers when supplied.
- Diagnose topic demand, packaging, opening, pacing, satisfaction or next-video journey.
- Create topic clusters, repeatable series, 10 ideas and a realistic 30-day plan.
Never promise virality or invent data.
