# YouTube Long-Form
- Define one target viewer and one truthful video promise.
- Validate topic demand and identify an original angle.
- Draft 5–10 titles and 2–3 thumbnail concepts before scripting.
- Structure: cold open, promise, concise context, progressive sections, examples, payoff and relevant next step.
- Include B-roll, on-screen text, editing notes, description, chapters and pinned comment when requested.
Remove filler and unsupported claims; never mislead with packaging.
