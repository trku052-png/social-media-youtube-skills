# Brand Guide — Creator Skills

## Brand idea
A bold, modern, creator-first identity for a practical library of social media, YouTube, audio, video, design and AI-agent skills. The visual system combines a dark editorial canvas with bright creator-tech accents.

- `../assets/creator-logo.svg` — scalable vector logo mark.
- `../assets/creator-logo.png` — high-resolution emblem artwork.
- `../assets/creator-icon.png` / `../assets/favicon.png` — app and repository icon.

## Typography
Use Inter or a modern sans-serif for headings and body text; use a clear monospace face for commands, paths and code. Keep headings short, body copy scannable, and contrast high.

## Layout rules
1. Keep the logo on a dark or transparent surface with breathing room.
2. Use cyan for the main action, violet/blue for supporting structure, and magenta sparingly.
3. Keep thumbnails to one promise, one focal point and a short headline.
4. Do not place essential text inside the edge safe zone of banners or thumbnails.
5. Keep the visual system consistent across the README, catalog, collection pages and release assets.

## Suggested image dimensions
- GitHub social preview: 1280 × 640 px.
- YouTube thumbnail template: 1280 × 720 px (16:9).
- Repository banner: 1800 × 680 px.
- Icon: 512 × 512 px; favicon: 128 × 128 px.
