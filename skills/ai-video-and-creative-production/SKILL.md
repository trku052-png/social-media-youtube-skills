# AI Video and Creative Production
- Confirm deliverable, platform, aspect ratio, duration, style, brand, and available tools.
- Choose AI-assisted stages: ideation, storyboard, b-roll, captions, cleanup, voiceover, or translation.
- Create prompts with subject, action, setting, camera, lighting, composition, and continuity.
- Preserve consistency across shots and request human review for artifacts and accuracy.
- Check rights and consent for faces, voices, music, logos, and source assets.
Deliver storyboard, shot list, prompts, edit notes, and quality checklist.
Never claim media was rendered, edited, or published unless the tool action completed.
