# Creator Monetization and Community
- Clarify audience needs, creator expertise, business model, and geography.
- Evaluate services, products, memberships, affiliate partnerships, sponsorships, and platform programs.
- Assess fit, delivery effort, margin, disclosure duties, and eligibility.
- Draft an offer and value proposition.
- Outline a sponsor media kit using verified audience data only.
- Create comment, DM, moderation, and escalation guidelines.
- Define conversion and retention metrics.
Disclose paid partnerships where required. Never fabricate audience size, testimonials, or income.
