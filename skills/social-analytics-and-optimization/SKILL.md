# Social Analytics and Optimization
- Request platform, period, goal, and data export or metrics.
- Validate dates, units, denominators, and comparability.
- Segment by platform, format, topic, audience, and traffic source.
- Compare to account medians and relevant cohorts, not universal benchmarks.
- Diagnose exposure → view → retention → engagement → profile action → conversion.
- Recommend tests with hypothesis, variable, success metric, and review date.
Never fabricate missing data or infer causation from a small sample.
