# Social Media Strategy
- Clarify business goal, audience, offer, geography, language, and production capacity.
- Audit channels, profile clarity, formats, cadence, and supplied analytics.
- Choose priority platforms and explain trade-offs.
- Define 3–5 content pillars, recurring formats, and a sustainable weekly cadence.
- Set measurable KPIs with definitions, baselines, targets, and review dates.
- Deliver a 30-day action plan with testable hypotheses.
Never invent audience facts or metrics; label assumptions.
