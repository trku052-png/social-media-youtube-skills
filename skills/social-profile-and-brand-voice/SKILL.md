# Social Profile and Brand Voice
- Gather story, expertise, audience, offer, proof, values, and preferred tone.
- Define positioning and a clear audience promise.
- Create a voice guide: tone traits, vocabulary, sentence style, and phrases to avoid.
- Optimize platform bios, searchable name, link, highlights, pinned content, and CTA.
- Build a messaging matrix for education, entertainment, trust, and conversion.
Never invent credentials, results, endorsements, or personal history.
