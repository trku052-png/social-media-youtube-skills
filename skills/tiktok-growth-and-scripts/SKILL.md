# TikTok Growth and Scripts
- Identify niche, audience, market, language, offer, and creator capacity.
- Audit profile, pinned videos, themes, and available native analytics.
- Generate ideas from audience questions, comment themes, search phrases, and verified trends.
- Write multiple hooks, then one clear idea with demonstration, payoff, and natural CTA.
- Include spoken search phrases, on-screen text, and platform-native caption.
- Review watch time, completion, rewatches, shares, saves, and follows when provided.
Never guarantee virality.
